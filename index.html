Privacy Policy

Gravity Shift · Last updated 10 September 2026

The short version. Gravity Shift does not have accounts, a server, or analytics. Your puzzle progress is stored only on your own device and is never sent anywhere.

The one exception is advertising: the game shows ads through Google AdMob, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects some data in order to do that. That is the only data this app causes to leave your device.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Android application Gravity Shift (package com.gravityshift.gravity_shift), referred to below as "the app". Contact details are at the bottom of this page.

What is stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, in its own private storage. None of it is transmitted, and nobody but you can read it:

  • Which levels you have completed, your best move count and star rating for each, and how many attempts you have made
  • Your current level, hint balance, and total time played
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, reduce motion, on-screen buttons, theme
  • Timings used to pace adverts, so you are not shown two in quick succession

Deleting the app removes all of it. You can also erase it at any time from Settings → Delete my data inside the game.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address, or name is ever requested
  • No analytics or usage-tracking SDK is present
  • No crash-reporting SDK is present
  • No contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, or precise location access is requested
  • No data is sold, and none is shared with anyone other than as described below for advertising
  • The game is fully playable offline

Advertising

The app shows adverts supplied by Google AdMob. Two kinds appear: short videos you may choose to watch in exchange for a hint or an extra undo, and full-screen adverts between levels.

To select and measure adverts, and to detect fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information including your device's advertising identifier, technical details about your device and app version, your IP address (from which an approximate, city-level location may be derived), and how you interact with an advert. This processing is carried out by Google, not by the app's developer, who receives only aggregate earnings reports containing no information about individual users.

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and its Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Reset or limit your advertising ID. On Android, under Settings → Privacy → Ads, you can reset the identifier or delete it entirely, which stops it being used to personalise adverts.
  • Consent. Where the law requires it — including the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland — Google's consent tool asks your permission before showing personalised adverts, and you may decline. You can change that choice later from within the app's advert settings prompt.
  • Erase local data. Settings → Delete my data in the game, or simply uninstall it.

Children

The app is rated for ages 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. It is not knowingly used to collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, uninstalling it removes everything stored locally; there is no server-side record to delete.

Your rights

Because the app's developer holds no personal data about you, there is no developer-held record to access, correct, or export. Requests concerning data processed by Google for advertising should be directed to Google using the links above, or exercised through your device's advertising settings.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or a jurisdiction such as California with comparable rules, the rights those laws give you apply to Google as the party processing that advertising data.

Changes

If the app starts collecting anything further — analytics or crash reporting, for example — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
